## Part 2 — Database

- **Status:** live, running, local PostgreSQL 16.2, schema applied.
- **Exact table count:** 16 relations in the `public` schema = **15 approved tables + Alembic's own `alembic_version` bookkeeping table**.
- **Exact table names:** `schools`, `departments`, `programme_categories`, `programmes`, `programme_modes`, `programme_faqs`, `programme_features`, `applicants`, `applications`, `alumni`, `graduate_profile_requests`, `partner_enquiries`, `contact_messages`, `newsletter_subscribers`, `admin_users` — no `news`, `vacancies`, `employers`, `graduates`, `application_documents`, `audit_log`, `roles`, `permissions`, or any other speculative table.
- **FK verification:** 11 foreign keys, matching the Step 2 §29 ERD exactly — `departments.school_id→schools`, `programmes.school_id→schools`, `programmes.department_id→departments`, `programmes.category_id→programme_categories`, `programme_modes/programme_faqs/programme_features.programme_id→programmes`, `applications.applicant_id→applicants`, `applications.programme_id→programmes`, `applications.programme_mode_id→programme_modes` (nullable), `alumni.programme_id→programmes` (nullable). `graduate_profile_requests`, `partner_enquiries`, `contact_messages`, `newsletter_subscribers`, `admin_users` remain standalone, exactly as specified.
- **Constraint verification:** 10 `CHECK` constraints present, matching Step 2 §27/§28 exactly (`programmes.programme_type`, `applications.application_type`, `applications.status`, `graduate_profile_requests.status`, `partner_enquiries.enquiry_type`, `partner_enquiries.status`, `contact_messages.status`, `alumni.graduation_year` range). 5 email/slug/reference-code `UNIQUE` constraints present (`applicants.email`, `alumni.email`, `newsletter_subscribers.email`, `admin_users.email`, `applications.reference_code`, plus `schools.slug` and `programmes.slug` and `programme_categories.slug`).
- **Index verification — partial, flagged honestly:** every `PRIMARY KEY` and `UNIQUE` constraint has its automatic index (24 indexes total), which covers the email/slug/reference-code lookups Step 2 §26 called out. **However**, Step 2 §26 also recommended several plain (non-unique) indexes — on `programmes.school_id`/`department_id`/`programme_type`, `programme_modes.programme_id`, `applications.applicant_id`/`programme_id`/`status`/`submitted_at` — and the Step 3 model files never actually declared those as explicit `db.Index()`/`index=True` entries, so they are **not** present in this migration. This isn't a data-integrity problem (all FK constraints and uniqueness rules are enforced regardless), but it's a real gap against §26's own recommendation, not silently glossed over here: adding those as a small follow-up migration would be a low-risk, additive fast-follow, not a redesign — flagged for your decision rather than added unilaterally in this step.

## Part 3 — Seed Data

Sourced via a new `germaine-backend/scripts/extract_frontend_data.js` (reads `assets/js/school-data.js` and `assets/js/ai-courses-data.js` only, via Node, and never modifies them) feeding `germaine-backend/scripts/seed_catalogue.py` (single transaction, commits only on full success). Counts below are **actual counts queried back from the database after seeding**, not just in-memory tallies:

| Table | Expected (from source files) | Actual (seeded) | Match |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| schools | 15 | 15 | ✅ |
| departments | 11 (hospitality 3, engineering 3, cosmetology 3, business 2) | 11 | ✅ |
| academic programmes | 339 | 339 | ✅ |
| AI Bootcamp programmes | 14 | 14 | ✅ |

| programme_features | 42 | 42 | ✅ |

`programmes` total = 353 (339 + 14), confirmed by direct query. `schools.count` (declared in the source file) matched `len(programmes)` for all 15 schools during extraction — the same internal consistency Step 0.5/Step 2 already found, re-verified here at the row level. All 9 active schools (`is_active=true`, matching `SCHOOL_ORDER`) and 6 legacy schools (`is_active=false`) were seeded, per Step 2 §6's "9 active + 6 legacy-kept-for-bookmarks" design. Academic pricing was seeded as the flat `schools.fee` display string exactly as stored in source (never converted to numeric, per §39 item A). AI Bootcamp pricing: `programme_modes.fee_amount` is `NULL` for 0 of 32 modes (every mode in the current data has a real fee) and `programme_modes.start_date` is `NULL` for 9 of 32 modes — those 9 correctly represent the source data's "Contact Admissions" sentinel, stored as `NULL` rather than a literal string, exactly as designed.

**Note on a false-positive check:** a query for `slug ILIKE '%short-course%'` returned 3 rows — these are legitimate, verified-source academic programmes that happen to live inside the `journalism` school's real programme list in `school-data.js` (e.g., "Photography Short Course," a real KNEC-recognized offering within that school), **not** the six standalone `courses-short-course-*.html` pages referenced in §39 item C. Those six pages were never read by the seed script and nothing was seeded on their behalf.
