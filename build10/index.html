Discover Germaine Training Institute

Practical Skills. Real Careers. Bright Futures.

Germaine Training Institute (GTI) is Kenya's home for grounded, competency-based technical and vocational training that turns classroom knowledge into real industry experience, organized and supervised by our institute experts and industry partners. From National Diplomas to Certificates, Artisan and Vocational courses to Short Courses and Professional programmes, we are empowering young adults with hands-on training that fuels Health, Hospitality, Engineering, Media and Entrepreneurship with skilled talent. Every graduate leaves competent, innovative and work-ready to meet the dynamic demands of the 21st-century job market. Discover the programmes, people and possibilities waiting for you at Germaine.
