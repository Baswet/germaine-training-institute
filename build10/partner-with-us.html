The Germaine Advantage

Why Partner with Germaine

Six reasons organisations across Kenya choose to build their talent pipeline with us.

  1. Industry-Aligned Training

    Every programme is built on CBET standards and reviewed with employers, so what we teach is what your sector actually needs.

  2. Work-Ready Graduates

    Trainees are assessed in real workshop conditions and leave with the discipline, safety awareness and professionalism employers expect on day one.

  3. Practical Learning

    Over 100 practical workshop hours per programme across engineering, ICT, health, hospitality, agriculture, media and design.

  4. Innovation & Research

    Our Innovation Hub gives partners a testbed for applied projects, prototypes and community-focused problem solving.

  5. Strong Employer Network

    Join 400+ organisations already hosting attachments, recruiting graduates and shaping our training agenda.

  6. Long-Term Collaboration

    A dedicated Partnerships Office manages every agreement, so the relationship keeps delivering year after year.
