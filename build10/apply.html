Scholarship Guidelines

  1. The scholarship entitles one to study Diploma, Certificate or Craft Certificate.
  2. Diploma takes a minimum of 8 terms or 2 years.
  3. Certificate takes a minimum of 5 terms or 1 year 3 months.
  4. Craft Certificate takes a minimum of 4 terms or 1 year.
  5. The scholarship may be renewed after every 2 terms or 6 months of study.
  6. Kindly note the scholarship does not cover accommodation and meals during study.
  7. Virtual/Distance Learning & Talented students will get a 30% discount on the admission fees.
  8. Talent scholarship provides financial aid for students with exceptional abilities in sports and performing arts.
