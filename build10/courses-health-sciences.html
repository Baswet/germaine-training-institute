School of Medical & Health Sciences

The world population is increasing coupled with an ageing population drives demand for medical and health related professionals. Thus the need for qualified healthcare support workers has never been in doubt. The Germaine School of Medical & Applied Health Sciences (SMAHS) offers practical, affordable and accessible training for Orthopedic and Trauma specialists, Perioperative Theatre Technologist, Health Care Assistants, Caregivers and Homecare Managers, who provide vital support in hospitals, clinics, homes and care facilities. We offer diploma and certificate courses in various specializations.
