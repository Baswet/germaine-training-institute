Sports & Recreation

The Institute has robust football, basketball, rugby, volleyball and martial arts teams currently playing in the Kenya Women Premier League, Kenya Rugby Union, Kenya Basketball Leagues, FKF Division One League, Kenya Colleges Sports Association (KCSA) League among other tournaments.

The football men’s and ladies’ teams are the reigning KCSA champions having won the 2024–25 titles while the basketball ladies team are the current defending champions of National Colleges Championship in Kenya.

Other sporting activities that students engage in include scrabble, chess, and table tennis, among others.

The institute offers enhanced scholarship programme for talented students in various sports discipline.

Health & Counseling

Our Health and Counseling Centre offers professional medical care and confidential counseling. We also educate students on health and emergency procedures for a safer campus.

Beyond physical care, our counselors provide a private, judgement-free space where students can talk through stress, personal challenges, or the everyday pressures of college life. It's part of building a campus where every student feels seen, supported, and able to grow with confidence — emotionally as well as academically.
